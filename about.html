The engineering club is a space to create, think, and innovate.

The club is composed of four sectors: software engineering, mechanical engineering, design, and electrical engineering. Each sector is led by a student leader and supported by instructors who provide guidance and mentorship.

Our mission is to bestow each member with the powers, skills, and knowledge to change the world.

The club is open to all students, regardless of experience or background. We welcome anyone with a passion for engineering, problem-solving, and creativity.
